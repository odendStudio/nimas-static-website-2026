My Journey in UI/UX Design and Digital Experience

UI/UX Design is my passion, and I have extensive experience crafting intuitive, user-centered digital experiences. My journey began with graphic and web design , transitioning fully into UI/UX design. I’ve worked across startups, midsize companies, and enterprises in diverse industries, including software, networking, advertising, recruitment, insurance, e-commerce, and fintech. Currently, I’m a Senior UI/UX Designer at FLEXOPTIX GmbH , focusing on usability, workflows, and digital product optimization. I collaborate closely with developers and product teams to create impactful experiences.

My basic knowledge of software and website development enhances communication with developers, bridging the gap between design and implementation. My background in Advertisement Management has deepened my understanding of business strategy and marketing, helping me balance business needs, technological possibilities, and user expectations. I thrive on problem-solving, ensuring designs are both functional and engaging, while continuously refining my approach through sketches, mockups, and prototypes. My experience in team leadership has honed my abilities to effectively guide and motivate teams, fostering collaboration and ensuring projects are delivered successfully and efficiently.
